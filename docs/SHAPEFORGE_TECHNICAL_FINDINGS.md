## Path from input to saved mesh

GeneratorWindow.UpdateSourceShapes, RefreshPieces and DrawPiece derive eligible renderers, filtered shapes and piece status. GeneratorAssets.FindBody and Validate support discovery and explicit selection. LayerFit snapshots each renderer before assignment, in the body renderer's local space. FitSource reads the selected shared mesh shape, establishes an affected query region, and builds rest and deformed triangle search trees. ShapeTransfer.Fit transforms garment points into that space, projects them onto a rest triangle and interpolates its three displacement vectors. TransferField.Sample can refine this with distance-weighted or RBF motion from a cached patch grown over source mesh edges. A failed or undersupported patch can fall back to the triangle estimate. Both alternatives inherit the chosen starting face.

Fit fades distant matches, rotates part of the clothing's normal offset and maps motion back with the inverse linear transform. FitConnected relaxes displacements along welded edges, applies straight-span constraints and corrects contact against the fitted body. PreserveCompactDetails assigns a common translation to eligible small closed islands. LayerFit.Correct samples interactions with other pieces, then the body. FitPreview supports weight preview, picking, stroke history, local smooth/clearance and saving draft offsets. GeneratorAssets.Generate accepts a draft, a prepared layered fit or a direct transfer. ShapeTransfer.BuildMesh clones the input mesh, adds a frame and expands mesh bounds. GeneratorAssets persists a unique asset with GeneratedMeshInfo linking to the original, assigns the mesh under Undo, copies existing renderer weights by shape name, and records prefab modifications. On generation failure it reverts the group and cleans up its newly created asset. Restore and SaveRemainingShapes handle provenance and selective removal. Success-path Undo can restore a renderer while a generated asset remains on disk.

Clone-and-append retains base positions, vertex attributes, bone weights, bind poses and prior shape frames on that path. Renderer bones and rootBone are not reassigned. Preserve this contract through restore, export, reload and preview edits. Existing weights are carried by name, so changing indices alone should not change active weights. A production regression fixture should verify variable influences, split vertices, all frame channels, renderer references and prefab overrides after save/reopen.

## Ranked findings

1. **Authored intermediate motion disappears.** ShapeTransfer.ReadSourceDeltas (442–483) evaluates only weight 100 and BuildMesh (238) serializes one frame at 100. A source which pushes sideways at 50 and upward at 100 loses the sideways movement. A source which returns to rest at 100 can appear inactive. Ascending positive source weights are required, a last weight below 100 is extrapolated and later frames above the evaluation point are discarded. Even a linear source can require nonlinear cloth clearance. Preserve authored knots first and evaluate residuals between them. Frame-aware preview, correction storage, cache keys and restore are part of that change.

2. **An inner layer can move after its collision surface was captured.** LayerFit's constructor builds Motion and Fitted at 50 and 100 before Correct changes Result. ApplyPushes writes Result only. An outer jacket therefore sees the old shirt even if the body pushed the shirt outward. The final body pass can also invalidate an earlier garment correction. Refit affected trees from accepted motion between passes, remeasure all affected pairs, stop on tolerance or a pass cap and report residuals. Mere repetition against the original snapshots cannot converge on the final geometry.

3. **The closest face controls the entire estimate.** ShapeTransfer.Fit (144) and TriangleSurface.FindClosest/Search minimize distance before checking orientation. The subsequent normal agreement compares the source triangle to its deformed self. A cuff beside a torso can inherit torso motion even with excellent agreement. ClosestBarycentric uses the usual vertex, edge and face regions and the same coordinate ordering as the displacement sum. No coefficient-order bug was apparent. Test shared edges, degenerate faces and deterministic ties. Prefer several local candidates only where nearest alternatives imply different motion. Use adjacent garment matches as evidence and soften orientation scoring for double-sided fabric.

4. **Coincident coordinates are treated as sewn fabric.** FitConnected maps exact Vector3 positions to the first index before building edges. LayerFit.Correct also keys pushes by position. This helps UV splits, but two disconnected stacked panels touching at one position receive a shared displacement. Nearly coincident seam duplicates can remain disconnected. Preserve an explicit distinction between seam partners and separate shells. Build seam links from matching boundary edges, incident topology and compatible motion. Test both a split seam that must stay closed and touching panels that must slide independently.

5. **Clearance samples can miss crossings and create unnecessary lift.** Piece.Constrain uses signed face-plane distance, checks at 50 and 100, rejects unfavorable orientation and protects originally outside contacts. Correct samples vertices, edge midpoints and centroids. A narrow body triangle can cross between those samples, and a grazing contact can change closest face. Existing penetration is skipped on this path. The maximum-magnitude push selected per position need not satisfy competing normals. Record baseline intersections separately, then use localized triangle intersection diagnostics on swept candidate bounds before introducing more automatic push. Bound total outward travel and flag ambiguous open surfaces.

6. **Reference-state assumptions are implicit.** LayerFit uses sharedMesh vertices and source deltas, independent of other live renderer weights. A garment authored around an active body shape will match against the wrong body surface. Expose an explicit authored reference snapshot, resolve shape names, and compute source-to-target motion relative to it. Inverse transfer requires known authored endpoints and reversed correspondence where appropriate. Negating a nonlinear forward correction is insufficient. Preserve original garment geometry at its declared neutral slider state and clearly specify any reversed slider mapping.

7. **Shading loses twist within the tangent plane.** CalculateShading groups by exact base position and imported normal, computes rest/deformed incident normals and rotates the imported tangent with the normal. This respects many UV splits and hard edges. A triangle rotating within its own plane can keep its normal while its texture direction rotates. A glossy normal map can consequently shade incorrectly. Compare transported per-UV tangent directions to a UV-derivative reference, orthogonalize to the new normal, retain tangent handedness and fall back on degenerate UVs. Keep custom base normals. Source normal-only blendshape animation is also omitted by GetBlendShapeFrameVertices with null shading channels.

8. **Rigid details and scale have limits.** PreserveCompactDetails translates qualifying islands, so a buckle may remain level as its strap rotates. Its size/closedness heuristics miss flat ornaments and a standalone single-island buckle. Edge relaxation preserves local displacement smoothness but does not enforce edge lengths, dihedral angles or enclosed volume. Body-local Euclidean distance changes neighbor ranking under nonuniform body scale. The determinant guard and correct point/vector conversion handle invertibility, but do not establish world-metric invariance. LayerFit uses determinant sign for garment winding. Test mirrored, rotated, nonuniform and differently skinned renderers, including combined body shape and bone pose. A common bind-space fit cannot ensure identical posed motion when existing skin weights differ.

9. **An optional sync schema change can prevent mesh generation.** ModularAvatarSync.Bindings validates the outer array while TargetsShape, Matches and Apply access several child properties directly. A renamed field could throw. Matches also treats a two-key remap with identity endpoints as identity without checking curve tangents. Validate field layout up front, treat unknown curves conservatively and allow mesh generation with a clear sync-skipped result. Test existing user-owned bindings and partially supported schemas.

## Phase 2 measurements and remaining limits

The final Unity build retains the authored midpoint on a synthetic shape that moves laterally at 50 and returns to rest at 100. All three methods match the expected baked trajectory within 1e-5 mesh units. Neighbor agreement selected the moving surface on the synthetic near-tie where distance alone selected the stationary one. Candidate scoring uses the neighboring source normals and motion and keeps the original face when evidence is weak. Whole garment regions attached consistently to the wrong surface remain a difficult case.

On the Midnight bikini/leotard fixture the joint fit had 0 sampled inter-garment penetrations among 6,270 evaluated samples at each of 50 and 100, on both the predecessor and final build. The final independent-piece fit had 70 at 100 with a worst measured depth of 2.88 mm. This demonstrates the value of processing pieces together. It does not demonstrate a reduction versus the predecessor on this fixture. The measured bridge deviation at 100 was 2.439 mm for both builds. The three-piece fit took a median 6,870 ms over five runs versus 3,155 ms for the baseline on this workstation. These are editor observations under ambient machine load. Extra source knots require additional fits and delta storage. Endpoint-only single shapes remain one generated frame.

Visual review used Unity renders of the slip, shirt, hoodie and qipao in multiple views, including a posed view. The fitted slip and qipao show substantial intersections already at rest with the visible unmasked body. The renders support inspection of silhouettes and folds but cannot establish universal clearance. The private fixtures use one avatar family. An independent second-avatar validation and a glossy production normal-map comparison remain outstanding.

The layer pass has a three-iteration cap and reports when continuing motion reaches it. Preview additionally tests both directions of edge/surface intersections, ignores pre-existing segment hits, and marks newly detected crossings. Coplanar overlaps, multiple hits on a single edge and between-sample slider events can escape detection. Structural correction is bounded and local. It is not a volume solver. UV tangent transport falls back when no usable UV direction exists. Explicit reference modes use mesh-space blendshape positions and existing skinning, so different cloth/body skin weights may diverge in animation. Reverse transfer covers the authored 100-to-0 interval. Negative authored frame weights remain unsupported.
